Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Revamped Tabletop Games LLC ("we," "us") collects through our mobile games, this website, and when you contact us; how we use and share it; how long we keep it; and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect a username, your email address and a password, which we store only in encrypted (hashed) form. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive from that service an account identifier, the email address it shares with us and, depending on the service and what you allow, the name and profile picture you have there; we never receive your Apple or Google password. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, Apple gives us a private relay address instead of your email address, and the emails we send you reach you through Apple. We don't show your name or picture from these services in our games: your username, picture and color there are the ones you choose. You can add more than one of these sign-in methods to the same account. When you create an account, we also ask your age; we keep only whether you are 13 or older, not your birth year.

Profile. Your profile is your username (which does not need to be unique), the picture and color you choose, and a friend code that lets other players add you as a friend. One account and profile is used across all of our games.

Single-player games. A single-player game in progress, its moves and your settings are saved only on your device. If you are signed in, the results of each single-player game you finish (such as scores, placings and the other totals your statistics and achievements are calculated from) are saved to your account, so your statistics and achievements are the same on every device where you sign in. Games in progress and individual moves are not uploaded, except as part of an error report (see Error reports below).

Online games. When you play online, we store the data needed to run multiplayer games in the cloud: your username and profile, every move made in the game and when it was made (including moves made for you by a computer player if you run out of time or leave), results and match history.

Friends. We store your friends list, friend requests, invitations to games, the players you have recently played online with, the players you block and any reports you make about other players. While a game is open, we also record when you were last online and which of our games you are playing, so your friends can see it.

Analytics and diagnostics. Our games collect information about how they are used and how they perform, such as game events, session length, crash reports, device model, operating system and app version, language, an app or device identifier, and approximate location (country or region) based on your IP address.

Advertising. In games that show ads, our advertising partners may collect your IP address, device information, information about the ads you see and tap and, only where you allow it, your device's advertising identifier (the Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID). On iPhone and iPad, we ask for your permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites.

Purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive a confirmation of what you bought so we can deliver it, but we never receive your payment card details.

Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Feedback and problem reports. If you send feedback from a game, we receive what you write and the kind of message (a problem, an idea or something else), the game and its version, and a random identifier for your installation. Unless you switch it off in the feedback form, we also receive your device's model, operating system and version, screen size and language. If you are signed in, the message is linked to your account; if not, it is sent without your name.

Error reports. When something goes wrong in one of our games (for example, an error in the game, the game closing unexpectedly, or a game falling out of step with our servers), the game sends us an error report so we can find and fix the problem, unless you turn this off in the game's settings. An error report contains a description of the error and where in the game it happened; the game and its version; your device's model, operating system and version, screen size and language; a random identifier for your installation; your game settings; the game you were playing at that moment (for a single-player game, its current state and the moves of the current hand; for an online game, which game it was); and a record of what the game did shortly before (such as the screens you opened and the moves sent to and from our servers). If you are signed in, the report is linked to your account; if not, it is sent without your name. Our online game servers also record errors that happen during online games, with the game and the players in it.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and run our games, including saving progress and running multiplayer matches
  • To keep your statistics and achievements the same on all your devices
  • To let you add friends, see when they are online and invite them to games
  • To deliver purchases and restore them on new devices
  • To understand how our games are played, fix bugs and improve our games
  • To improve our computer players, including by training artificial intelligence (machine learning) models on the moves and results of online games. Before we use games for this, we replace your username, email address and account identifier with a random code.
  • To show ads, which may be personalized only where you allow it
  • To prevent cheating, fraud and abuse, and to keep our services secure
  • To respond to your messages and send important service notices
  • To comply with the law

How we share information

  • Other players in your online games can see your username, picture and color, your game moves and results. Players who add you as a friend can also see when you were last online and which of our games you are playing. Your profile is not shown to anyone else, and there is no way to search for players by name.
  • Service providers that host our servers and databases or provide analytics, crash reporting and advertising process information on our behalf. We will list each provider used by a game in this policy, with a link to its privacy policy, before releasing a game that uses it. Our providers are:
    • Supabase: accounts and sign-in, databases, file storage and server functions
    • Resend: sending account emails, such as the codes to confirm your email address or reset your password
    • Fly.io: running our online game servers, which host online games and record their moves, and hosting test versions of our games
  • Apple and Google receive information as needed to distribute our games and process purchases and, when you choose to sign in with them, to sign you in.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our players, our company or others.
  • Business transfers: if our company is merged or sold, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information for money. Some privacy laws treat sharing information with advertising partners for personalized ads as a "sale" or "sharing." You can opt out of that as described under Your choices and rights.

How long we keep information

  • Account, profile, friends, multiplayer data and finished single-player game results are kept, linked to your account, until you delete your account.
  • Online games used to improve our computer players are kept under a random code instead of your username, email address or account identifier. We delete the link between that code and your account no more than 14 months after the game, as soon as you choose Anonymize my data, or when you delete your account. After that, the games can no longer be connected to you, and we may keep them to train and test our computer players.
  • Online status (when you were last online and which game you are playing) is replaced each time it changes; we keep only the latest.
  • When you delete your account, we delete or permanently anonymize your personal information within 30 days. We keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as tax and purchase records, and anonymized game statistics that can no longer identify you.
  • Deleting a game from your device removes the data saved on that device. It does not delete your account or the data saved to it in the cloud, because we are not notified when a game is uninstalled. To remove that data, delete your account.
  • Analytics data is kept in identifiable form for no more than 14 months, then deleted or combined into totals that cannot identify you.
  • Support messages are deleted when we no longer need them to help you.
  • Feedback and problem reports are kept for no more than 2 years, then deleted. If you delete your account, the reports linked to it are deleted with it.
  • Error reports are kept for no more than 1 year, then deleted. If you delete your account, the reports linked to it are deleted with it. Reports waiting on your device to be sent are deleted if you turn error reports off.

Your choices and rights

  • Anonymize your data from the game's settings (Settings, then Account, then Anonymize my data). Your name, picture and color are removed from other players' history of the online games you have played (you appear as "Former player"), you are removed from their list of recent players, and the games kept to improve our computer players can no longer be connected to you. Your account, friends, purchases and your own statistics and achievements stay. This cannot be undone. Games you play afterwards are linked to your account as usual until you choose it again.
  • Delete your account from the game's settings, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Turn off error reports in the game's settings (Settings, then Send error reports). The game then stops sending them and deletes any waiting to be sent.
  • Block or report a player from your friends list or the list of players you have recently played with.
  • Limit ad tracking: on iPhone and iPad, go to Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. On Android, go to Settings, then Google (or Privacy), then Ads, to delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Access or correct your information by emailing us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights. Residents of some U.S. states may have the right to know, access, correct and delete their personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also have the right to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We rely on these legal bases: providing the service you requested (accounts, friends, multiplayer and syncing your statistics and achievements), our legitimate interests (analytics, security, preventing cheating and improving our computer players), and your consent (personalized ads and tracking), which you can withdraw at any time. To make a request, email us. We may need to verify your identity, and we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Where information is processed

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

Children

Our games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. When you create an account, we ask your age. If you are under 13, you can still play single-player games, but you cannot create an account, and we do not keep your answer. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account or sent us personal information, we will delete it. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and hashed passwords. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will also notify you in the game or by email before it takes effect.
