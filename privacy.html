Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Revamped Tabletop Games LLC ("we," "us") collects through our mobile games and this website, and how we use it. In short: our games are built to work without collecting your personal information, and we never sell personal information.

Information our games collect

Our games do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts, and they do not require an account. Game progress and settings are stored on your device and are deleted when you delete the game.

Our games do not currently include advertising, analytics or third-party tracking. If a future version adds any third-party service, we will update this policy to name the service and describe what it collects before that version is released.

Purchases

Purchases in our games are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment card details. Apple and Google send us sales reports that do not identify individual players by name.

Information collected by Apple and Google

When you download or buy a game, Apple or Google may collect information under their own privacy policies. See the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond to you and to fix problems you report, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for that purpose.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Our games are meant for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us. Because our games do not collect personal information, in most cases we hold none. We will respond to requests as required by the privacy laws that apply to you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
